GBR digital landscape audit

GBR inherits a service network, not a collection of websites.

Passengers encounter common needs across a distributed estate of national services, operator brands, infrastructure information, specialist suppliers, accounts and operational platforms.

The executive conclusionA single GBR brand can simplify how the landscape feels, but only if service continuity survives the boundaries underneath.

Metrics use different evidence layers. The reconciliation view explains each denominator and prevents double-counting.
